## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-precision, utilitarian workspace optimized for mobile productivity and document digitization. Built around an editorial clarity and corporate-modern aesthetic, the interface prioritizes documents over decoration. 

The emotional signature is dependable, razor-sharp, and effortless. Interfaces must feel crisp, immediate, and tactically organized. Structural elements remain light and unobtrusive, allowing digitized paperwork, metadata labels, and actionable workflows to surface with utmost clarity.

## Colors
The color system operates on an ultra-clean, white-first canvas. Backgrounds remain grounded in cold, pale neutrals while interactive states utilize vivid, controlled violet hues.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Violet (`#6D5CE7`)**: Interactive hero elements, primary actions, floating triggers, and selected document bounds.
- **Deep Violet (`#5948CB`)**: Pressed states, active filters, and high-emphasis focal items.
- **Soft Lavender (`#EEEAFE`)**: Subtle chip backgrounds, active row fills, and tint highlights behind primary icons.
- **Light Blue (`#DDF2FF`)**: Information calls, secondary status badges, and processing states.
- **Secondary Blue (`#2584BD`)**: Export flows, cloud sync cues, and complementary utility links.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Document card canvases, bottom sheets, toolbars, and input containers.
- **Canvas Neutral (`#F6F7FB`)**: The base mobile background layer.
- **Primary Text (`#242238`)**: High-contrast, dense typographic hierarchy for document titles and critical data.
- **Secondary Text (`#77758A`)**: Timestamps, page counts, subheaders, and neutral utility icons.
- **Border Neutral (`#E8E7F1`)**: Hairline dividers, card outlines, and document grid delimiters.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (`#22A06B`)**: Successful OCR export, cloud sync completion, auto-edge detection lock.
- **Warning (`#E9A23B`)**: Storage thresholds, low-light scan warnings, perspective skew alerts.
- **Error (`#D94D5C`)**: Scan failures, unreadable OCR zones, destructive deletion actions.

## Typography
Typographic scale balances the structural precision of Manrope for headers and the neutral legibility of Inter for continuous reading, file paths, and metadata.

- **Manrope**: Applied strictly to major document folders, modal sheet headers, and page view counters to impart a modern architectural quality.
- **Inter**: Applied systematically to file directories, scan dates, tags, tabular metadata, form fields, and OCR reading panes.
- **Scale Rules**: All numeric measurements (page counts, file sizes, OCR confidence) leverage tabular lining numerals for precise alignment across vertical lists.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a mobile-first, strict 4px/8px incremental grid designed for dense information presentation without visual fatigue.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile Grid**: 4 columns with `12px` gutters and `16px` outer margins. Document grids default to a 2-column card view or a 1-column list view.
- **Tablet Grid**: 8 columns with `16px` gutters and `24px` outer margins, facilitating split-screen editing panes and two-up document review flows.
- **Desktop/Tablet Landscape**: 12 columns with a persistent left rail navigation (`280px` width) and dynamic content panes.

### Padding and Density
- Compact component padding (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) maintains high information density for page indexes, tag selectors, and action sheets.
- Modal bottom sheets and floating capture controls preserve `space-lg` to `space-xl` edge zones to avoid touch overlap with system gesture bars.

## Elevation & Depth
The system achieves depth primarily through planar separation and hairline borders, utilizing ambient shadows solely to detach active or floating elements from the scan plane.

- **Level 0 (Surface Base)**: Neutral background `#F6F7FB`, completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Card & List Rows)**: White `#FFFFFF` surfaces defined by a crisp 1px solid `#E8E7F1` border. No drop shadows.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Menus, Sticky Bars)**: White `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E8E7F1` paired with a delicate ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(36, 34, 56, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Scan Shutter Buttons, FAB)**: Soft violet-tinted elevation: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(109, 92, 231, 0.18)` for primary triggers, or `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(36, 34, 56, 0.12)` for sheet overlays.
- **Camera Viewfinder**: Zero-depth, full bleed overlay with 2px stroke high-contrast `#6D5CE7` corner guides and semi-transparent dark masks (`rgba(36, 34, 56, 0.4)`).

## Shapes
The design uses a balanced radius hierarchy (`roundedness: 2`, 8px standard) to convey tactile usability without feeling childlike or overly toy-like.

- **Standard Radius (`8px`)**: Applied to document thumbnails, text fields, contextual menus, and default buttons.
- **Large Radius (`16px`)**: Applied to bottom sheet cards, document preview containers, and batch-action docks.
- **Pill Radius (Full Curve)**: Reserved strictly for category chips, tag badges, status indicators, and the floating camera capture button.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#6D5CE7` background, `#FFFFFF` Inter bold text, `8px` corner radius, `44px` minimum tap height. Active state transitions to `#5948CB`.
- **Secondary**: `#EEEAFE` fill, `#6D5CE7` text, no border. Used for tertiary actions such as "Add Page" or "Filter View".
- **Outline**: Pure white surface, 1px border `#E8E7F1`, `#242238` text.
- **Floating Shutter (Camera)**: Large 72px pill/circle, multi-ring design with `#FFFFFF` core ring and `#6D5CE7` inner trigger, equipped with Level 3 elevation.

### Document Cards & List Items
- **Grid Card**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E8E7F1` border, 8px radius. Features an aspect ratio thumbnail (4:3 or A4 ratio) with page count badge pinned to top-right (`rgba(36, 34, 56, 0.75)` fill, white text). Lower third displays title, modification date, and 3-dot overflow trigger.
- **List Item**: Full-width white row, 1px bottom border `#E8E7F1`, 48px square thumbnail on the left, two-line text hierarchy (Title in `title-md`, date/size in `body-sm`), trailing batch select checkbox or chevron.

### Chips & Filters
- Compact height (`28px`), full pill roundedness.
- Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#E8E7F1` border and `#77758A` text.
- Active state: `#EEEAFE` fill, 1px `#6D5CE7` border, and `#6D5CE7` text with an optional trailing dismiss cross.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Inset `#FFFFFF` fields with 1px `#E8E7F1` borders, 8px radius, `40px` height.
- Focus state switches border to 1.5px `#6D5CE7` with zero outer glow.
- Left-aligned utility icons rendered in `#77758A`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Checkbox: 20px rounded square (4px radius), 1.5px border `#E8E7F1`. Checked state fills `#6D5CE7` with crisp white check mark icon.
- Selection Overlay: Document selection triggers a subtle `#EEEAFE` overlay tint over the entire document card.

### Scanner-Specific Tooling
- **Edge Adjustment Handles**: 16px circular white nodes with 3px `#6D5CE7` border, accompanied by a dynamic 2x magnification loupe during touch interaction.
- **Status Indicator Badges**: Subtle light pill container (`#DDF2FF` for cloud synced, `#EEEAFE` for OCR ready, `#E8E7F1` for local draft) paired with `label-sm` bold typography.
